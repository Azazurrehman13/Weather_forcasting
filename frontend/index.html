You can enter just one level if that's all you have. Blank levels are estimated from your nearest entered level(s), smoothly fading back to the training average the further away they are (in log-pressure) — every estimated value is flagged in the results, and it's a rougher guess the fewer levels you enter.

Check against live weather (optional, uses the lat/lon above)

What this actually checks. Open-Meteo has no free ground-truth upper-air data for past dates -- so this fetches Open-Meteo's CURRENT conditions as input, runs our model, and compares our forecast against Open-Meteo's OWN forecast (ECMWF/GFS) for the same future times. It's a model-vs-model sanity check, not accuracy against observed reality. Only levels both sources share are compared (others are skipped, not interpolated).
